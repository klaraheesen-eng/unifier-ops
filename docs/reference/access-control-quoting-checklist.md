# Access Control Quoting Checklist

Use this checklist when qualifying and quoting access-control jobs, especially gates, pedestrian access points, biometric readers, intercoms, electric locks, and mag locks.

## Locking strategy first
Before choosing hardware, confirm how the opening will physically lock and what structure the lock will fix to.

### Mag lock rule
- Do **not** assume a mag lock is suitable just because the client asks for one.
- A mag lock should ideally fix to a **solid, stable frame or structure**.
- Avoid quoting a mag lock that effectively pulls against another moving or insufficiently rigid gate leaf or secondary panel.
- If the opposing structure is not rigid enough, the mag lock will be unreliable and can fail prematurely or break over time.
- If the gate/frame arrangement is uncertain, ask for drawings, photos, and installer confirmation before finalizing the lock type.

### Must-confirm questions for mag lock suitability
1. Will the mag lock fix to a **proper fixed frame**, column, or rigid support?
2. Is the opposing gate leaf or bracket stable enough to handle repeated magnetic holding force without movement or flex?
3. Is one leaf passive/fixed and one active, or are both leaves moving regularly?
4. Would an electric lock, shear lock, strike, drop bolt, or alternative locking arrangement be better than a mag lock for this opening?
5. Who is responsible for the gate fabrication and lock mounting provisions?

## Core qualification questions
1. Is the opening pedestrian, vehicle, or mixed use?
2. Single leaf, double leaf, sliding, swing, door, turnstile, or gate?
3. Which leaf/panel is active and which is passive?
4. What credential types are required: face, fingerprint, card, PIN, QR?
5. Is the requirement access control only, or also attendance/time reporting?
6. Is visitor communication needed: intercom, camera, indoor monitor, remote release?
7. What is the cable distance and must Unifier supply cable/conduit/trunking?
8. Is power already available, and is backup power required or optional?
9. Indoor, outdoor, or semi-outdoor environment?
10. Is there a fixed mounting surface for the reader/intercom and locking hardware?

## Site evidence to request
- Photos of the opening from both sides
- Gate or door fabrication drawings if available
- Close-up photos of hinges, latch area, frame, pillars, and adjacent walls
- Cable route photos or plan
- Distance estimate from opening to control point / reception / power source

## Quote discipline
- If lock suitability is uncertain, quote the system subject to final site confirmation.
- Avoid overcommitting to a mag lock unless the fixing structure is clearly appropriate.
- Where relevant, separate optional items like backup power, protective shields, and upgraded reader models.

## Draft Response
Hi Danny

Yes, we can assist.

For your setup, a simple and reliable solution would be a small IP CCTV system with remote viewing, recording, and mobile app access.

Recommended setup:
- 5 x IP cameras, one in each storeroom and one covering the packing / dispatch area
- 1 x 8-channel NVR with hard drive for continuous recording
- Remote viewing on phone and laptop from overseas
- Playback by date and time so you can check stock movement and confirm what was packed per invoice

Why this would suit your site:
- The rooms are small, so one properly positioned wide-angle camera per room is normally enough
- The extra camera at packing/dispatch is important if you want to reduce packing mistakes and verify what left against an invoice
- The system stays practical and easy to manage without overcomplicating the site

Budget estimate:
- 4-camera option: from approximately R18,000 to R24,000 ex VAT installed
- 5-camera recommended option: from approximately R22,000 to R29,000 ex VAT installed

This would normally include:
- Cameras
- NVR recorder
- Hard drive storage
- Power / POE and accessories
- Installation and setup
- Remote viewing configuration
- Basic user training

Important notes:
- Final pricing will depend on cable distances, mounting points, internet availability, and whether after-hours installation is required at the co-working space
- CCTV can help you monitor stock movement and visually check stock levels, but it will not do automatic stock counting unless a more advanced inventory solution is added later

If you would like, we can arrange a quick site assessment and then issue a formal quote.

Kind regards

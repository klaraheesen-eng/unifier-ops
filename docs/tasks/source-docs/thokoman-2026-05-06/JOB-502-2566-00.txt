INSTALLATION PREPARATION GUIDELINES

Before Our Team Arrives:

Site Access & Walk­through: Please ensure someone is available at the installation site to provide access and conduct an initial walkthrough with our installation team.
Power Requirements: Ensure stable electricity is available at the installation site. Each switch, DVR/NVR or power supply requires a dedicated power outlet.
DVR/NVR Location: Please identify a secure, dry location for the DVR/NVR that has adequate ventilation, access to power, and is protected from unauthorized access.
Internet Access: If your system includes remote viewing capabilities, please have wired (ethernet) internet access available, preferably close to the DVR/NVR location.
Clear Work Areas: To speed up the installation process, please ensure that work areas are accessible and free from obstacles.
Mobile Device: Please have the main cellphone or tablet that will be used to access the CCTV system available. Our technicians will assist with setting up the Hik­Connect app for remote
viewing.

On the Day of Installation:

Our team lead will be your primary point of contact during the installation. Please communicate any specific requirements or concerns directly with them on arrival.
Our team will introduce themselves and confirm the installation plan before starting.
Once work is complete, a final walkthrough will be done, and you'll be required to sign off on the job card.
If our team needs to return to complete the installation due to unforeseen circumstances or site­related issues not identified in advance, additional charges may apply.

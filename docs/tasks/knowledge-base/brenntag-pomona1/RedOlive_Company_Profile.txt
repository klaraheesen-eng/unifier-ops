About Us
RedOlive Estates Pty Ltd, trading as Unifier, is a South African consulting business focused on
helping companies improve operations, strengthen processes, and grow sustainably through
practical, affordable, and personalised support. Since 2015, the company has provided consulting
services across multiple industries while also managing the day-to-day operations of Unifier CCTV.
Our work is grounded in a hands-on approach, strong client relationships, and a genuine
commitment to helping businesses thrive. We believe that expert support should be effective,
accessible, responsive, and aligned to the realities of each client's business.

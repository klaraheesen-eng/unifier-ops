## Section 3 - Health and Safety Assessment

### 3.1 General
Required / requested information:
- Whether there is a person responsible for Safety, Health and Environmental matters
- If yes, provide name, surname, and email address
- ISO accreditations, if applicable:
  - ISO 9001
  - ISO 18001
  - ISO 14001
  - Other
- If no HSE management system exists, whether one is planned and by when
- Trade or professional body membership, if applicable, with copy
- Contractor scheme membership, if applicable, with details
- Medical fitness certificates, not older than one year
- Copies of IDs / work permits with clear pictures for all personnel coming to site
- WCL2 templates

### 3.2 Management System and Performance
Required / requested information:
- HSE Policy (attach copy)
- Accident statistics for previous 3 years, including:
  - Average number employed
  - Fatalities
  - Major injuries / reportable injuries
  - Lost work day injury less than 3
  - Minor injury
- Details of work-related fatalities in last five years, if any
- Details of work-related reportable accidents or incidents in last 12 months, if any
- Details of fines / investigations by Department of Labour or other law enforcement in last 12 months, if any
- Environmental Policy (attach copy)
- Details of fines / investigations by environmental authorities in last 12 months, if any
- Accident / incident reporting system details and copy if possible
- Near Miss reporting system details and copy
- Details of regular Health and Safety meetings and last 3 meeting minutes
- Drugs and Alcohol Policy (attach copy)
- Emergency contact numbers
- All applicable HSE appointments
- Copy of OHS Act and other applicable regulations

### 3.3 Training
Required / requested information:
- Training plan for all employees (attach copy)
- Whether training records are maintained, with applicable training certificates
- General Health and Safety related training, with recent certificates
- Training categories, where applicable:
  - First Aid
  - Manual Handling
  - Chemical Hazard Awareness
  - Confined Space Entry
  - Fire Fighting
  - Safe Working at Height
  - Safe Use of Work Equipment
  - Safe Use of Electrical Tools
  - Avoiding accidents from Slips, Trips and Falls
  - Other training
- Regular Tool Box Talks, with 3 examples
- Competency assessments, with recent example
- If agency staff are employed, how their competency is assessed

### 3.3 Subcontractors
Required / requested information if subcontractors are used:
- Subcontractor policy / supplier approval process
- How safety performance of subcontractors is assessed
- How Brenntag safety information is communicated to subcontractors

### 3.4 Task Management
Required / requested attachments and details:
- Method Statements for each work to be undertaken, including work at Brenntag
- Risk Assessments for work to be undertaken on Brenntag site
- How risks and method statements are communicated to staff before work starts
- Confirmation that suitable PPE is provided
- PPE Register
- Equipment certificates including load tests, calibration, service, etc.
- How safety performance is monitored on site
- Procedure for reporting and investigation of incidents / injuries and corrective action
- HSE Plan
- Fall Protection Plan for height work
- Copies of relevant authorities’ approvals where applicable
- Relevant inspection registers and checklists

Face Recognition Terminal

Face recognition terminal adopts deep learning algorithm, which helps to recognize the face faster with higher accuracy. It
also supports multiple authentication modes: face/card/fingerprint/PIN authentication, etc. It can be applied in multiple
scenarios, such as buildings, enterprises, financial industries, and other important areas.

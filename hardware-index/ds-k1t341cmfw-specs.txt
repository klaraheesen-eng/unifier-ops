Key specs:
- Product type: Face recognition terminal with fingerprint, card, and PIN authentication
- Display: 4.3-inch IPS capacitive touch screen
- Camera: Dual-lens, 2 MP wide-angle, 720P video stream
- Capacity: 3,000 faces, 3,000 cards, 3,000 fingerprints, 150,000 events
- Recognition: Face recognition < 0.2 s per user
- Additional features: mask-wearing detection, QR code recognition, face anti-spoofing, Hik-Connect support
- Audio: Two-way audio, noise suppression and echo cancellation
- Network: RJ-45 10/100 Mbps self-adaptive, plus 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 802.11b/g/n
- Interfaces: lock control, exit button, door contact input, tamper, RS-485, Wiegand, USB
- Controller: built-in access controller
- Power: 12 VDC to 24 VDC, 2 A
- Power consumption: ≤ 10 W
- Working temperature: -30 °C to 60 °C
- Protection: IP65
- Suitable applications: buildings, enterprises, financial sector, other controlled access areas

Optional accessories from datasheet:
- DS-KAB6-D1 base bracket
- DS-KAB6-ZU1 face terminal bracket
- DS-KAB671-P2 stand stick
- DS-KAB34X-S1 protective shield

Quoting relevance:
- Stronger field option than face-only terminals where fingerprint and card fallback are desirable.
- IP65 and optional protective shield make it a better fit for semi-outdoor or exposed pedestrian gate installations.
- Two-way audio support may help when combining visitor communication and access verification flows.
- Built-in controller plus lock/exit/door contact interfaces suit a single controlled gate or door.
- RS-485 to secure door control unit is useful for higher-security installs where terminal damage should not release the door.
